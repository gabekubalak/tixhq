# KrattOS — Campaign Image Prompts

Copy-paste these into ChatGPT (or any image model). All three share
the visual DNA of the hero image: solarpunk kitchen, fridge-sized
sage-green steel + oak + brass cabinet, four LED-lit shelves, glass
door, composter at base, brass plumbing, art-nouveau kitchen with
golden-hour light. The wordmark "KrattOS" appears on the brass
nameplate at the top of the door.

For every render: generate 3–4 takes and pick the one where the
**KrattOS** wordmark is spelled cleanly. If it garbles, regenerate
with "redraw with the KrattOS wordmark spelled correctly and no
other text."

---

## A. Square 1:1 social-share crop (for IG / Twitter / GFM card)

Use this when you want a single tight portrait of the appliance with
the brand legible at thumbnail size.

```
Create a hyper-realistic, photographic product portrait for a
crowdfunding campaign social-share card. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

THE APPLIANCE:
- A fridge-sized indoor vertical-farming cabinet, roughly 1.85 m tall
  and 70 cm wide, photographed straight-on, slightly cropped at the
  base so the four illuminated growing shelves dominate the frame.
- Powder-coated sage-green steel frame, warm oak wood trim, brushed
  brass accents and food-grade tubing climbing the side. Large
  tempered-glass door revealing the interior.
- Inside, four shelves stacked vertically. Each glows with warm
  full-spectrum LED grow light. From top to bottom: tiny microgreens
  sprouting in trays, young basil seedlings, mature butterhead
  lettuce nearly ready to harvest, a fourth tray of mizuna.
- Small character LCD and a red latching mushroom emergency-stop
  button visible to the right of the door.
- Composter unit at the base, partially visible.

BRANDING:
- The wordmark "KrattOS" on a brushed brass nameplate at the very
  top of the door, modern geometric sans-serif, crisp and legible.
  Spelled exactly K-r-a-t-t-O-S. No other text anywhere.

ENVIRONMENT:
- Solarpunk kitchen background, softly out-of-focus. Climbing vines
  and terracotta pots framing the edges. Warm golden-hour light
  filtering in from the right. Art-nouveau iron window detail just
  visible behind.

PHOTOGRAPHY:
- Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, f/2.8. Eye-level, dead-on
  three-quarter angle. Realistic reflections in the glass, true-to-
  life leaf texture, warm overall colour grade.
- Editorial product-photography quality. Tight 1:1 framing so the
  cabinet fills 80% of the square.
```

**Recommended use:** GoFundMe campaign card image. Instagram post.
LinkedIn announcement post. Profile-image-style avatar of the
appliance.

## B. "Door open, hands harvesting" hero for the *what your money builds* section

Use this when you want the image to tell a *use* story rather than a
*product* story. This is the emotional shot that converts donations.

```
Create a hyper-realistic, photographic editorial image for a
crowdfunding campaign's "what your money builds" section. The mood is
warm, human, hopeful. 16:9 landscape.

THE SCENE:
- A fridge-sized indoor vertical-farming appliance standing in a
  sunlit solarpunk kitchen, exactly as in the hero render: powder-
  coated sage-green steel, warm oak trim, brushed brass plumbing,
  four illuminated growing shelves visible through a large tempered-
  glass door.
- The glass door is OPEN, swung to the left, revealing the four
  glowing shelves of fresh produce inside in full detail.
- A pair of human hands — relaxed, mid-30s, no rings, slightly soil-
  marked — reaches into the second shelf from the top and gently
  cradles a head of mature buttercrunch lettuce, about to lift it
  free of the tray. A small wooden harvest basket sits on the
  counter nearby, already holding a bunch of basil.

DETAILS:
- The interior is alive: dewdrops on leaves, soft warm LED grow-
  light wash on the hands and on the lettuce. Tiny food-grade
  silicone tubes visible feeding each tray.
- The composter at the base is partly visible behind the lower
  shelf — it tells the story that scraps will go in as the lettuce
  comes out.

BRANDING:
- The wordmark "KrattOS" still visible on the brass nameplate at the
  top of the open door. Spelled exactly K-r-a-t-t-O-S. No other
  text anywhere.

ENVIRONMENT:
- Solarpunk kitchen in warm golden-hour light. Climbing vines
  framing the upper corners. Art-nouveau iron-framed window in the
  soft-focus background. A ceramic plate on the counter waiting for
  the harvest.

PHOTOGRAPHY:
- Shot on a full-frame camera, 35mm lens, f/2.8. Slightly low angle,
  looking up into the open cabinet to make the appliance feel
  generous and welcoming. Shallow depth of field with the hands and
  the lettuce in sharp focus, the rest beautifully soft.
- Photojournalistic, editorial, warm grade. Magazine-quality. The
  feel of a New York Times Magazine food feature.
```

**Recommended use:** mid-page hero in the GoFundMe story, right above
the "What your money builds" funding-bucket table. This is the image
that turns interested readers into donors.

## C. Schematic-style cutaway for the *how it works* section

Use this when you want to *explain* the appliance instead of sell it.
Less photoreal, more elegant editorial illustration — like a
*Popular Science* or *Apartment Therapy* infographic.

```
Create a clean, elegant editorial cutaway illustration of a fridge-
sized indoor vertical-farming appliance. The style is a mix of
mid-century technical-manual line work and modern solarpunk
infographic — soft warm palette, gentle shading, hand-rendered
quality. Not photorealistic. 16:9 landscape.

LAYOUT:
- Three-quarter front view of the cabinet, with the front-right
  wall and door cut away cleanly (a slight diagonal "peel" reveal)
  so the entire interior is visible.
- The cabinet is the same KrattOS appliance: 1.85 m tall, 70 cm
  wide, powder-coated sage-green steel frame, warm oak trim,
  brushed brass plumbing, glass door (here drawn as a cut edge).

INTERIOR (labelled):
Show and label, in elegant editorial typography:
- "Camera" — small pan/tilt camera on the upper-left interior wall
  watching the shelves
- "Edge-AI brain" — a small Jetson-style board mounted to the back
  wall behind the top shelf
- "LED grow panel" — a glowing horizontal panel under each shelf
- "Growing tray" — four labelled stacked trays with plants at
  visibly different growth stages (microgreens → seedlings →
  mature lettuce → mizuna)
- "Moisture probe" — a slender probe in one tray with a tiny
  call-out
- "Fan" — circular fan icon at the back of one shelf
- "Nutrient slurry tubing" — thin brass-coloured pipes running
  from the composter at the base up to each shelf
- "Composter" — the unit at the base, labelled, with a small
  arrow showing kitchen scraps going in
- "Slurry tank" — adjacent tank with level indicator
- "Safety contactor" — small box at the bottom-right with an
  obvious heavy cable to the appliance
- "E-stop" — the red latching button on the front panel
- "Air-gap (no internet)" — annotated with a small Wi-Fi symbol
  CROSSED OUT, sitting where a network port would be

BRANDING:
- The wordmark "KrattOS" rendered in modern geometric sans-serif at
  the top-left of the image, with a tagline below it: "The offline
  AI farm appliance." Spelled exactly K-r-a-t-t-O-S.

STYLE:
- Soft cream / off-white background, warm muted palette. Hairline
  strokes for the cut-edge of the cabinet. Each call-out label is
  set in a thin sans-serif with a delicate leader line to the part.
- Mood: confident, friendly, knowable. Should make a non-technical
  reader feel "ah, I get it." Like a Field Notes diagram or a
  Studio Ghibli technical sketch.
- Avoid: clinical CAD line drawings, dark backgrounds, neon, glossy
  3D-rendered look. This is the friendly explanation, not the
  beauty shot.
```

**Recommended use:** mid-page in the GoFundMe story under a "How it
actually works" heading. Also doubles perfectly as a 1-slide image
in the pitch deck's *Solution* slide.

## Quick generation checklist

For each prompt:

1. Generate 4 variations.
2. Check the KrattOS wordmark is spelled correctly. Reject any with
   "KratosOS," "KratOS," "KratiOS," etc.
3. Check the cabinet proportions roughly match the others. Hero,
   square, and open-door shots should look like the *same* product.
4. Crop and export at:
   - **A (square)** — 1080×1080 for IG / 1200×1200 for GoFundMe card
   - **B (open door)** — 1920×1080 for the GFM story body
   - **C (cutaway)** — 1920×1080 for the GFM story body and 1600×900
     for the pitch deck

Save all three under `assets/marketing/` once you've picked the
keepers.
