# Business / Fundraising docs

Materials for raising money and launching GroveOS as a product. Read
in order:

1. [`fundraising-roadmap.md`](fundraising-roadmap.md) — the 90-day plan
2. [`pitch-deck-outline.md`](pitch-deck-outline.md) — 10 slides to write
3. [`bom.md`](bom.md) — single-unit + at-scale cost of goods
4. [`unit-economics.md`](unit-economics.md) — margins, LTV, cash need

All numbers in `bom.md` and `unit-economics.md` are honest estimates
based on Q4 2025 retail pricing, not guaranteed quotes. Re-verify
with suppliers before they appear in any external document.

> Not legal, financial, or investment advice. Validate licensing,
> patentability, grant eligibility, and securities law (esp.
> Regulation D for the angel round) with a qualified attorney
> before acting on anything here.
